Information We Collect and Use #### a. Personal Information We **do NOT collect, store, or share** any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as: - Name, phone number, physical address, or email address (no user account registration or login required). - Financial information or payment details. - Contacts, photos, videos, or files from your device. #### b. Device Permissions & Network Usage Our application requests only the minimum necessary permissions: - **`android.permission.INTERNET`**: Required to fetch and update public health, safety, and environment (HSE) educational articles, safety guides, and examination materials from our public educational content source. - **`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`**: Used strictly to detect whether your device is connected to the internet, enabling offline caching and readable offline bookmarks. #### c. Local Data Storage on Device To provide a smooth learning experience, the app stores the following data **locally on your device only**: - Bookmarked HSE study articles and topics. - Mock test scores, quiz progress, and revision history. - App theme preferences (Light, Dark, or System) and reading font size. *This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to external servers.* --- ### 3. Third-Party Services The application may utilize the following standard Google and Android platform services: - **Google Play Services**: For app distribution, crash diagnostics, and performance monitoring. - **Public Educational Content API (Google Blogger API)**: To retrieve published HSE safety training modules and educational articles. These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies regarding how they handle technical metadata: - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) - [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) --- ### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance) Our application is designed for educational and professional training purposes (safety officers, students, and industrial professionals). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. --- ### 5. Data Security We value your trust. Because our app does not collect personal accounts or sensitive user data, your personal privacy is maintained. All locally saved quiz progress and bookmarks remain under your full control on your device. --- ### 6.
