Privacy Policy

Application: HSE Study Guide | Package: com.hsestudyguide.afjal
Developer: MD AFJAL ALI | Last Updated: October 04, 2026

HSE Study Guide ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by MD AFJAL ALI, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for our mobile application distributed on the Google Play Store.

1. Personal Information Collection

We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from users:

  • No Account Required: No login, signup, or registration is required to use any feature of the app.
  • No Personal Details: We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses.
  • No Financial Data: No payment details or banking information are collected or processed.
  • No Media/Files Access: The app does not access your photos, camera, microphone, or storage files.

2. Android Device Permissions

Our app requires only the standard and minimum network permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch and update public health, safety, and environment (HSE) educational articles, study guides, and mock examination questions.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to detect your device's connectivity status to enable seamless offline reading and bookmarking.

3. Local Storage on Device

To provide a smooth learning experience, the app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Saved offline bookmarks and favorite articles.
  • Mock exam practice scores, completed quizzes, and test progress.
  • Theme selection (Light/Dark/System) and custom font size preferences.

This data is stored inside the local SQLite database on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The application interacts with standard Google services for educational content delivery:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google Blogger API (for public educational content retrieval)

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is an educational and professional training resource designed for safety officers and students. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age.

6. User Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your local data. You can delete all your stored bookmarks and mock test history at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings > Apps > HSE Study Guide > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the application.
